Invoice Guides

How to Create an Invoice

Enter the seller's details, customer information, invoice number, invoice date and payment due date. Add a clear description of each product or service, along with the quantity and price. Check the calculated total before printing or saving the document.

What Should an Invoice Include?

A typical invoice includes the seller's name and contact details, customer details, a unique invoice number, issue date, description of goods or services, quantities, prices, applicable taxes, total amount due and payment terms. Legal requirements vary by country and business type.

How to Save an Invoice as a PDF

After completing your invoice, select “Print / Save as PDF”. In the print dialog, choose “Save as PDF” as the destination if your device supports it. Review the preview before saving.

How to Calculate Invoice Tax

Enter the applicable tax percentage in the tax field. SwiftInvoice calculates tax on the subtotal after the discount. Make sure the rate and calculation method are appropriate for your jurisdiction and business.

How to Keep Invoice Records

Keep copies of invoices and supporting documents in a secure location. Record payments and outstanding balances, and follow the record-keeping rules that apply to your business.
